Layman Knows Publisher

Layman Knows Publisher is the private publishing tool for Layman Knows, a YouTube channel that explains money and personal finance in plain language with everyday analogies.

It is used only by the channel's own team to upload finished episodes and Shorts to the Layman Knows YouTube channel and to set their title, description, thumbnail, captions, playlists and publish time. It is not a public app and does not access anyone else's account.

Layman Knows Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
