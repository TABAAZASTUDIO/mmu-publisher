MMU Publisher privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What MMU Publisher is

MMU Publisher is a private tool used only by the team behind the Medicine Made Us YouTube channel to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, MMU Publisher uses YouTube API Services to act on the Medicine Made Us channel only. It uploads videos, sets their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlists, publish time and content disclosures) and reads the channel's name and playlists to confirm it is publishing to the right channel.

What it stores

MMU Publisher keeps a log of the videos it has published (video IDs, titles and publish times) so the same video is never uploaded twice. Google sign-in tokens are stored only in the team's own automation account and are used only to publish to the Medicine Made Us channel. It does not collect data about viewers or any other YouTube user.

Sharing

MMU Publisher does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

MMU Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops MMU Publisher from acting on the channel.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the contact details on the Medicine Made Us channel page.
