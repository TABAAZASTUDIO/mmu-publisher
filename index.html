Medicine Made Us

MMU Publisher

MMU Publisher is the private publishing tool for Medicine Made Us, a documentary YouTube channel about the true stories behind the medicines that changed what humans can survive.

It is used only by the channel's own team to upload finished episodes to the Medicine Made Us YouTube channel and to set their title, description, thumbnail, captions, playlists, publish time and the altered or synthetic content disclosure. It is not a public app and does not access anyone else's account.

MMU Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
